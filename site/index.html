The board

Every asset in the top 200 carrying a reading right now. Price is judged on its excess over the universe median, not on its raw move, so a coin up 3% on a day the median coin is up 4% counts as falling behind. Stablecoins are excluded: every peg underperforms a rising market, and that is not a signal.

Against the market

Both lines are indexed to 100 at the start of the recorded window, so they share one axis and the gap between them is the excess return the scorecard grades. The strip underneath shows which reading was live at each snapshot.

The scorecard

Every reading is graded against what the price actually did, at +4h, +24h and +72h, on excess return over the universe median — beating zero in a rising market is not skill. Misses are shown. capitulation claims no direction, so it is counted and never graded.
